# OpenJetty Test Dataset – Immigration User Scenarios

## H-1B & Employment

1. I'm on an H-1B visa. My priority date is getting close to becoming current. I visited India six months ago and have a 5-year-old daughter. What happens if I lose my job next month?

2. I have been on H-1B since 2020 and my employer just announced layoffs. My I-140 is approved. Can I stay in the US if I get fired?

3. My H-1B expires in 10 months and my employer says they might not renew it. What are my options?

4. I changed employers last year using an H-1B transfer. Does that affect my green card process?

5. My employer filed PERM but I want to join another startup. Will I lose everything?

---

## Green Card & Priority Date

6. My EB-2 India priority date is March 2015. The visa bulletin moved forward this month. Can I finally file I-485?

7. I have an approved I-140 but my priority date is still far away. Is there anything I should be doing now?

8. I was born in India but my spouse was born in Canada. Can that help us get the green card faster?

9. My priority date became current while I was traveling outside the US. Did I miss my chance?

10. I accidentally entered the wrong priority date when filling a form. Is that a serious issue?

---

## Travel

11. I visited India for four months while on H-1B. Could that affect my green card timeline?

12. My passport expires next year and I'm planning to travel internationally. Should I renew before filing I-485?

13. I left the US while my change of status was pending. Did I abandon my application?

14. I traveled during STEM OPT and now I'm worried I made a mistake. What should I check?

15. I have Advance Parole but also a valid H-1B. Which should I use when re-entering the US?

## Family

16. I have a daughter who turns 21 next year. Will she still be included in my green card application?

17. My spouse is on H-4 and wants to start working. Can they apply for work authorization?

18. My son was born in India but has lived in the US since he was two. Does that change anything?

19. My wife recently got her own H-1B. Should we keep H-4 as a backup?

20. I got married after my I-140 approval. Can my spouse be added later?

## Job Changes

21. I changed jobs five months after my I-140 approval. Did I lose my green card process?

22. I switched employers after 10 years on H-1B. Does my priority date stay the same?

23. My startup is being acquired by another company. Will that impact my immigration status?

24. My employer reduced my salary because of budget cuts. Could that create visa problems?

25. I work remotely from another state now. Do I need to update anything with USCIS?

## F-1 / OPT / H-1B Transition

26. I graduated this year and I'm on OPT. My employer wants to sponsor H-1B next year. What should I be doing now?

27. My STEM OPT expires in three months and I wasn't selected in the H-1B lottery. What options do I have?

28. I accidentally worked one extra week after my OPT expired. How serious is that?

29. I want to start my own company while on STEM OPT. Is that allowed?

30. My H-1B wasn't selected and I'm thinking about going back to school. Would that let me stay legally?

---

## Complex Multi-Factor Scenarios

31. I'm on H-1B with an approved I-140. I changed jobs in August 2023, have a wife on H-4, and two kids in elementary school. My priority date is approaching. Did changing employers affect my green card?

32. I recently lost my H-1B job after working in the US for eight years. My wife works on H-4 EAD and we bought a house last year. How much time do I have to find another employer?

33. My priority date is almost current, but I accepted a remote job in another state. I also traveled to India for six months last year. Should I be worried?

34. I spent eight months outside the US taking care of my parents. My employer kept me on payroll. Will this affect my future citizenship eligibility?

35. My H-1B extension is pending, my passport expires in six months, and I need to travel urgently for my sister's wedding. What risks should I know about?

36. My daughter turns 21 in four months and our green card priority date is still not current. Will she age out of the application?

37. I have an approved I-140 from my previous employer, but I joined a startup after only four months. My new company hasn't started PERM yet. What should I do?

38. My wife and I are both on H-1B visas. She received an offer from another company while my employer is filing my I-485. Could our decisions affect each other?

39. I came to the US on F-1, completed OPT, moved to H-1B, and now my employer wants me to relocate to Canada for a year before bringing me back. How would that affect my green card?

40. I recently got divorced while my employment-based green card process is ongoing. My ex-spouse was on H-4 and our son is included in the application. What changes now?

---

## Intentional Edge Cases

41. I forgot to renew my passport before filing for H-1B extension. Is my application still valid?

42. I changed employers two weeks before my priority date became current. Can I still file I-485?

43. My employer says they will withdraw my I-140 because I resigned. Does that erase my priority date?

44. I have an approved I-140 but I was outside the US when layoffs happened. Can I still return?

45. My son was born in Canada, my wife was born in India, and I was born in Nepal. Which country determines our green card wait time?

46. I have been working remotely from India for the past four months while still employed by my US company. Could that impact my H-1B status?

47. I received an RFE on my I-140 while changing jobs. Should I respond or let my old employer handle it?

48. My H-1B grace period ends next week but I just received a verbal job offer. Am I safe?

49. My company is merging with another business and HR says immigration paperwork will transfer automatically. Is that always true?

50. My priority date is current today, but next month's visa bulletin may retrogress. Should I file immediately or wait?
